PRD — SYSTEMATIC CRYPTO STRATEGY & SIGNAL PLATFORM
Working Title: SignalEdge
Version: 2.0 — Systematic Strategy First

1. PRODUCT VISION

Build a crypto trading strategy platform inspired by the systematic approach of Crypto Wealth.

The product should NOT begin as another website producing hundreds of BUY/SELL signals.

Core model:

ONE SYSTEMATIC BITCOIN STRATEGY
-> HISTORICAL BACKTEST
-> WALK-FORWARD VALIDATION
-> OUT-OF-SAMPLE TESTING
-> PAPER TRADING
-> LIVE TRACK RECORD
-> REAL-TIME SIGNALS
-> TRANSPARENT PERFORMANCE

The first objective is to determine whether one clearly defined, reproducible strategy can generate positive risk-adjusted returns over long periods after realistic trading costs.

Signals are the delivery mechanism. The strategy and its measurable track record are the product.

Never promise guaranteed profits.

2. PROBLEM TO SOLVE

Many crypto signal websites focus heavily on:
- Win rate
- Number of signals
- Percentage accuracy
- Multiple take-profit targets
- High leverage
- Large individual trade percentages

These can look impressive while the underlying strategy fails to compound capital consistently.

The core PulseCharts problem is:
"Signal accuracy does not necessarily translate into long-term profitability."

The new product must answer:
"If a user followed this exact strategy using the documented risk, position-sizing, fee and execution assumptions, how would their capital have behaved over hundreds of trades and multiple market cycles?"

3. CORE PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

PRINCIPLE 1 — STRATEGY FIRST
Start with one systematic BTC strategy, not many signal types.

PRINCIPLE 2 — REPRODUCIBILITY
The same market data + strategy version + parameters must produce the same signals.

PRINCIPLE 3 — PROFITABILITY OVER ACCURACY
Primary metrics:
- Net return
- Profit factor
- Expectancy
- Average R
- Maximum drawdown
- CAGR/annualized return
- Risk-adjusted performance
- Losing streaks
- Equity curve
- Monthly/yearly returns

PRINCIPLE 4 — COST-ADJUSTED RESULTS
Include:
- Trading fees
- Spread
- Slippage
- Funding where applicable
- Execution delay assumptions

PRINCIPLE 5 — NO CHERRY-PICKING
Losing trades and losing periods remain visible. Signals cannot be deleted because they performed badly.

PRINCIPLE 6 — OUT-OF-SAMPLE VALIDATION
A strategy that only works on historical data is not validated.

PRINCIPLE 7 — CONTROLLED RISK
Prefer a robust strategy with reasonable returns and controlled drawdown over an extraordinary backtest dependent on extreme leverage or overfitting.

12. WALK-FORWARD VALIDATION

Use chronological data.

Example:
Training: 2020–2023
Validation: 2024
Out-of-sample: 2025
Paper trading: 2026
Live: only after validation

The strategy must be evaluated on data not used to develop or optimize it.

If performance collapses out-of-sample, the strategy is NOT production ready.

13. STRATEGY VALIDATION GATES

A public premium strategy must pass:
1. Reproducible backtest
2. Positive expectancy
3. Positive profit factor
4. Realistic fees
5. Realistic slippage
6. Acceptable maximum drawdown
7. Sufficient trade count
8. Multiple market regimes
9. Walk-forward validation
10. Positive out-of-sample performance
11. Paper-trading confirmation
12. No obvious look-ahead bias
13. No obvious parameter overfitting
14. No dependence on one exceptional period

Failure means STATUS = RESEARCH.
